About Me!

Hi! I’m a recent graduate from Mohawk College’s Software Support program, where I learned how to build apps that work well and look good too. My front-end journey actually started in Mohawk’s Graphic Design program through the UX/UI stream, playing around with HTML and CSS. Now, I mix what I’ve learned from both programs to make websites that are not just functional but also fun.

I’m a pretty laid back creator who loves putting a bit of personality into every site I make. Thanks to my background in the arts and my web development know-how, I get to solve problems creatively and enjoy the process along the way. I am eager to leverage technical proficiency and artistic talent in a dynamic design-focused role.

When I'm not learning about web development or graphic design, I volunteer for the Hamilton Literacy Council as Literacy Tutor; helping adults learn how to read. I enjoy taking short walks in nature and taking care of my house plants. I sometimes watch K-Dramas and C-Dramas with my family. But my long time favorite hobby is painting. Whether it's with watercolors or digitally with Krita or Infinite Painter.
