About Me!

Hi! I’m a recent grad from Mohawk College’s Software Support program, where I learned how to build apps that work well and look good too. My front-end journey actually started in Mohawk’s Graphic Design program through the UX/UI stream, playing around with HTML and CSS. Now, I mix what I’ve learned from both programs to make websites that are not just functional but also fun.

I’m a pretty laid back creator who loves putting a bit of personality into every site I make. Thanks to my background in the arts and my web development know-how, I get to solve problems creatively and enjoy the process along the way.
